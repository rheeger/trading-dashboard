📖 How DSL Phases Work

🟡 Phase 1 — Accumulate: The position is building. A wide trailing stop (3% retrace from high-water mark) protects against catastrophic drops, but gives room to breathe. The absolute floor (your SL) is the hard backstop. Needs 3 consecutive breaches to close — one dip won't kill it.

🟢 Phase 2 — Protect: Activates when uPnL hits the first tier (10%). The trailing stop tightens to 1.5% and tier floors ratchet up — once profit is locked at a tier, it can't be lost. Only needs 2 breaches to close. Higher tiers lock more profit and have even tighter stops.

🎯 Tiers: Each tier triggers at a uPnL % and locks a floor. E.g., Tier 1 triggers at +10% and locks +5%. Once locked, even if price retraces, the position closes at the tier floor — not at a loss. Tiers only ratchet up, never down.
